Frequently asked questions

Where are my notes stored?

In the folder you chose, as ordinary Markdown files. Shokonotes keeps no copy and no database of its own. Its small settings files (pins, favourites, folder symbols) live in a .shokonotes folder inside your notes folder.

How do I sync between Mac and iPhone?

Put your notes folder in iCloud Drive on the Mac, then choose that same folder on the iPhone. Shokonotes has no server of its own.

Can I edit notes inside Shokonotes?

On the Mac, you write in Shokonotes' own editor or in the external editor of your choice; Shokonotes picks up changes made elsewhere as soon as the file is saved. On iPhone you can capture new notes, and change an existing one by tapping Edit. Reading a note never shows a cursor.

What are the default keyboard shortcuts?

  • ⌃⌥⌘N — quick note into the Inbox, from any app
  • ⌃⌥⌘S — show or hide the library, from any app
  • ⌘N new note · ⌘F search · ⌘P print · ⌘0 show the library window

The global shortcuts can be changed in Settings ▸ General.

Does Shokonotes collect any data?

No. No account, no analytics, no tracking. See the privacy policy.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple: request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Requirements

macOS 14 or later, iOS 17 or later. One purchase includes both the Mac and iPhone apps.
