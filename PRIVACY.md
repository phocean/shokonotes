# Privacy policy — Shokonotes

Shokonotes is a local app for macOS and iPhone. Notes are files in a folder
you choose.

- The app does not collect accounts, analytics, or crash telemetry.
- The app does not send your notes to a server.
- A security-scoped bookmark of the notes folder is stored in UserDefaults
  so the sandbox can open that folder again.
- Pins, folder symbols and favourites are stored as JSON inside a
  `.shokonotes` directory at the root of your notes folder.
- Preview rendering is local (cmark).
- Dictation on iPhone, when used, runs on-device.
- The Home Screen badge is a local inbox count; it is not sent anywhere.
- Network access is not requested.
