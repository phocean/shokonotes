# Contributing

Bug reports and feature requests are welcome: open an issue, and say what you
did, what you expected, and your macOS or iOS version.

**Code contributions are not accepted for now.** Shokonotes is published under
the GPLv3 and also sold on the App Store. Only the copyright holder can ship
the code there, so every line has to stay his. If that changes, pull requests
will be accepted under a contributor agreement that grants him the right to
distribute the contribution under other terms, and this file will say so.

You are free to fork the project under the terms of the GPLv3.
